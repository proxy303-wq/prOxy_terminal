# Goodman Notes — The Crypto Trader (2nd ed), mapped to the Delta engine

Glen Goodman (2023/2nd ed), mined 2026-08-31 directly from the PDF
(page cites = printed pages). The single most relevant book for the crypto
side: a *trader's* manual with explicit rules, parameters and results.

## The headline (p. 104): "DAY TRADING IS FOR LOSERS — LITERALLY"

- "No, I'm not a bloody day trader!" (p. 104). Day traders pay ruinous fees
  (his example: £25,000 in fees), compete with professional HFT/hedge funds
  on an unfair playing field, and make split-second decisions that end in
  crashes (pp. 104–105).
- **This is the direct verdict on our 5-minute crypto engine.** The July A/B
  (every symbol −2% to −3%) is not a tuning problem — it's the wrong game.
- His four mantras (p. 114): **Trade the trend / Grow your profits / Cut
  your losses / Control the voices.**

## The two key rules (p. 75)

1. **GROW YOUR PROFITS** — hold winners through scary corrections (2017: a
   30% June–July drop, then a ten-bagger, p. 75); "buy a crypto when it's
   already going up... if it's a good trade, it will be making you money
   straight away" (p. 79).
2. **CUT YOUR LOSSES** — "A bunch of small losses have never troubled my
   bottom line. A few big losses most certainly have" (p. 63). No 50%
   drawdowns, ever ("I would pack it all in", p. 10).

## His strategy (trend following on DAILY charts)

- **Timeframe: daily charts** ("Generally I use daily charts ('D')",
  p. 125); zoom to 6h/4h only for entry precision.
- **The 200-day MA defines the long-term trend** (p. 119, 200–202): buy on
  the breakout above it (his 2015 example, pp. 200–201); **don't sell while
  price stays above the 200-day MA** — a trailing stop that exits when price
  dips below it (p. 202).
- **20-day × 50-day MA crossover system** (p. 203): 50-day defines the
  long-term trend, 20-day times entries — he backtests exactly this on BTC
  (TradingView strategy tester).
- Keep it simple: "no advantage in complicating things... some of the most
  successful trading strategies are relatively straightforward" (p. 126).

## Risk & position sizing (the numbers)

- **Risk 0.5–1% of capital per trade** ("successful traders tend not to risk
  more than 0.5 to 1%", p. 233) — our 0.2% is comfortably inside.
- **Stops = 1× to 2× ATR** ("I prefer 1 x ATR or 2 x ATR", p. 234); 3–4×
  gives more room but bigger losses. Place the stop at the natural level
  (e.g., below the failed-breakout low, p. 233).
- **Volatility-adjusted / risk-parity position sizing** (pp. 231–232): put
  more capital in calm cryptos, less in wild ones — size each position so
  every crypto risks the same amount. ATR-based sizing formula (p. 232).
- **Rebalance / take profits in chunks** — "selling a chunk of my position
  if it has risen a long way, to collect some profits" (p. 234).
- Pyramiding: tempting, but do it cautiously (p. 236).

## His results (honest framing)

- "Turning £3,000 into £100,000" (pp. 7, 60) — via SHORTING the 2008 crash
  (a once-in-a-generation event, not a repeatable monthly rate).
- Trend examples: holding BTC through 2016–17 = 6,000% (p. 76); 2014→2025
  hold = 35,000% (p. 18). Bull-market numbers — he's open that drawdowns of
  30%+ are normal within a trend (p. 75).
- The honest takeaway: his edge is trend-following across the bull-bear
  cycle with tiny risk per trade — NOT a high-frequency monthly yield.

## Top-10 actions for the Delta engine (Goodman edition)

1. **Kill the 5-minute crypto scalper** — the fees+noise math guarantees
   bleed (pp. 104–105); the July data agrees.
2. **Move crypto to DAILY bars** — 200-day MA trend filter (p. 202).
3. **Entries: 20/50-day MA cross or breakout above the 200-day MA** (p. 203).
4. **Stops: 1–2× ATR, not fixed %** (p. 234) — vol-adaptive, exactly what
   the Cryptoassets notes recommended.
5. **Risk 0.2–0.5% per trade** (already there at 0.2%, p. 233).
6. **Trailing exit: hold while price > 200-day MA** (p. 202) — the
   lock-profit layer becomes "never round-trip a trend".
7. **Staged profit-taking** (p. 234) — sell chunks, not all.
8. **Risk-parity sizing across symbols** (pp. 231–232) — equal-risk
   allocation instead of equal capital.
9. **Shorting: only with the trend, cautiously** (p. 78–79) — avoid
   counter-trend shorts (the 5-min engine shorts constantly; the daily
   system mostly longs).
10. **Accept the reality**: a trend system can sit in drawdown/inaction for
    months; the monthly "yield" question is the wrong frame for crypto.
